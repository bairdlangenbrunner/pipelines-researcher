# Owner and operator consistency: one entity, one spelling

Baird 2026-10-20, after Transco showed up as `Transco`, `Transcontinental Gas Pipeline`, `Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line Company, LLC` and five other spellings, and Texas Gas Transmission as `Texas Gas Transmission`, `Texas Gas Transmission LLC` and `Boardwalk Pipeline Partners`. Follows `2026-10-05_owner-style-normalization.md`.

## Where things stand

A scan of every `Operator` and `Owner<N>` cell on the 2026-10-19 snapshot of the operators/owners tab plus every pending staged value (11,139 cells, 1,858 distinct entities) finds 155 entities written more than one way, covering 3,564 cells. The current styler would unify 83 of them on its own. The other 72 need a ruling or a registry check. The biggest: Saudi Aramco (3 spellings, 324 cells), Enbridge (3, 174), Energy Transfer (3, 90), Kinder Morgan (2, 87), TC Energy (2, 84), QatarEnergy (2, 75), Transco (8, 73), Gulf South Pipeline (6, 24), Texas Eastern Transmission (4, 29), Columbia Gas Transmission (3, 30), Northern Natural Gas (3, 41).

## Why the first pass did not fix it

1. The 2026-10-05 style store (2,878 cells) was staged but never reviewed or pushed. Its review-app scope is still `new`, so the sheet still holds the old spellings.
2. The styler works one cell at a time. A bare name with no legal form (`Texas Gas Transmission`, `Transco`, `Enbridge`) is held as `no_legal_form` even when the same company's full name sits in the next cell or the next row. Nothing ever compared cells for the same company.
3. The style rules themselves allow two spellings. The doc says "Company stays with LLC", the team's legal-forms list has `Co LLC` as the short form, and per-company rulings went both ways (`Florida Gas Transmission Co LLC` on 10-14, `Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line Company LLC` today).
4. `restyle_staged_owners.py` only rewrites a staged value that differs from the sheet. A research line that carries the sheet's old spelling keeps it, and once the sheet is re-spelled `push.py` skips that line as "ref backs Owner1 'Transco', sheet now says …", so its refs are lost.
5. The lowest-level-owner rule (2026-10-16) was applied to new research only. Existing cells that name a parent were never swept: `Boardwalk Pipeline Partners` sits in `Owner1` on 21 Texas Gas Transmission, Gulf South and Gulf Crossing rows whose pipeline company is a Boardwalk subsidiary, and `Williams` / `Williams Companies` sits on Transco rows.

## Plan

1. **Rule.** One spelling per company, chosen in this order: the team's own spelling when the ownership export has the company (exact entity); else the legal name from a registry (SEC EDGAR, the state business registry, the FERC tariff) styled with the legal-forms list; else the bare name. LLC takes `Co LLC` (Baird 2026-10-20; the legal-forms list's short form, matching the 10-14 Florida Gas ruling), so `Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line Co LLC`. The gazetteer's exact spelling still wins. Done 2026-10-20 in `entity_style.py`, `owner_style.md` and the Transco rulings.
2. **Cluster tool.** `scripts/owner_consistency.py` groups every name cell (sheet, pending staged work, the style store) by company, using the stem, the alias file, and acronym or short-name pairs seen on the same row (`Transco` beside `Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line …`). Clusters whose spellings already style to one form are settled mechanically. For the rest the agent checks the registry and writes a ruling into `data/owner_rulings.json` with every variant. Look-alikes that are different companies (`Phillips 66` and `Phillips 66 Partners`, `Energy Transfer` and `Energy Transfer Partners`, `Kuwait Petroleum Corp` and a stray `Co`) are checked one by one and kept apart where they really differ. Only a cluster the registry cannot settle goes to Baird. Done 2026-10-20: about 220 rulings, each with its registry or company source, plus `data/owner_near_verdicts.json`, which records a verdict on every look-alike pair (`different`, kept apart, or `step3`). The report `notes/owner-consistency-2026-10-20.md` shows 180 groups: 95 ruled, 83 the styler settles on re-staging, 2 split (DCP Midstream LLC and LP are two companies; Dominion Transmission is a step 3 rename), and nothing left for Baird. Renames are never ruled as spelling. They go to step 3 with refs: Dominion Transmission to Eastern Gas Transmission and Storage Inc, Plains Midstream Canada ULC to Keyera Midstream Canada Ltd, Sabine Crossing LLC to Sabine Crossing Pipeline LLC, Sempra Energy to Sempra, the Alliance partnerships, Genesis on Kupe (Genesis Energy Ltd of New Zealand), and the merged or sold Energy Transfer Partners, Williams Partners, Phillips 66 Partners and Spectra Energy.
3. **Parent sweep.** For rows where `Owner<N>` names a parent of the company that holds the pipeline (Boardwalk over Texas Gas Transmission LLC, Gulf South Pipeline, Gulf Crossing Pipeline; Williams over Transco; others the scan finds), stage the subsidiary as the owner with a validated ref (FERC tariff, SEC filing, or the company's own page). These are research changes, so they carry refs and stamp `Researcher = CB`, unlike spelling-only lines.
4. **Re-stage.** Re-run `stage_owner_style.py` on a fresh snapshot with the new rulings so it supersedes the 10-05 store, which moves to `archive/`. Give `restyle_staged_owners.py` an `--include-carried` mode so pending research lines that carry an old spelling are re-spelled too.
5. **Apply.** Spelling-only lines go through the review app and `push.py` (plan, Baird's OK, `--apply`; they stamp `LastUpdated` only). Spelling-only lines are pushed before the research lines on the same cells so no ref line is skipped. Then the review page is republished.
6. **Keep it fixed.** `owner_style_lint.py` gains a consistency check that fails when the sheet plus pending staged work hold more than one spelling for a company. `check_shard_coverage.py` and gate O refuse a staged name that is a known variant of a ruled company. `owner_style.md` gets the one-company-one-spelling rule.
