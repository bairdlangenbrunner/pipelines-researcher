- **`verified_refs` is lenient with no verifications; `sweep_gates.verified` is strict — never mix them across a store/gate boundary (2026-09-10).** `merge_qc.verified_refs(urls, verifs)` treats a falsy `verifs` as "clean and blocklist-filter only" and returns **all** the URLs; `sweep_gates.verified(r)` requires `ok && contains_value` and returns none. Any writer that delegates to the helper while a gate applies the strict rule produces a store the gate disagrees with — that is exactly how `harvest_sentinel_findings.py` (the LAST writer of `__VALIDITY__`/`__REDUNDANCY__`, and until 2026-09-10 the only one importing no `merge_qc` at all) shipped gate-B/D findings the store had already been told to fix. - **Value-less sentinel records cannot fill `verifications[]`, so they QC to `medium` no matter how well sourced they are (2026-09-10).** The array's contract binds `contains_value` to a *value*, and a `__VALIDITY__`/`__REDUNDANCY__`/`__STATUS__` record is prose with `values: {}` — an agent writing one honestly has nothing to put in it.
