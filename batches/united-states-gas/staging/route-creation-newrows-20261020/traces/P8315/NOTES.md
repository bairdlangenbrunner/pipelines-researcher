This row is the Crossroads Pipeline Expansion Project, a proposed expansion with no stated length and no defined loop or extension.
The only map found is page 2 of the February 9, 2026 non-binding open season notice. It is a regional schematic that draws the existing Crossroads line in red from the Cygnet receipt point in Ohio to the Schererville delivery point in Indiana. It does not show any new pipe.
Baird asked (2026-10-21) for a trace of that red line anyway, as the best available route for this row. The trace is the existing mainline, not the expansion, and it should be read that way.
How it was made: the map's Lake Michigan, Saginaw Bay and Lake Erie water edges were matched to the Natural Earth 10 m lakes outline to fix the map scale and position (the match overlaps by 94 percent). Five shoreline points from that match are the ground control points in gcps.json. The Natural Earth state lines for Indiana, Michigan and Ohio land on the faint state lines drawn on the map, which is an independent check. The red line was traced per pixel column, with short gaps under the dots and arrows bridged straight.
Fit: five points, affine, root mean square error 0.18 km, leave-one-out 0.68 km. The traced line is 326 km, about 203 miles, which agrees with the roughly 200 miles East Daley gives for the existing line. The map's station dots sit 4 to 11 km from the real towns (La Paz 11 km, Defiance 6 km, Schererville 6 km, Cygnet 4 km), so treat the position as schematic, about 5 to 10 km.
