# P8241 SEMCO Keweenaw Connector Pipeline route notes

The source map is the Approved Route Plat, Attachment 1 to the Michigan Public Service Commission order of May 15, 2025, Case No. U-21780. The plat is pages 17 to 20 of the 22 page PDF in maps/P8241, from https://adms.apps.lara.state.mi.us/Mpsc/ViewCommissionOrderDocument/27138.

The four pages are the four segment sheets of the Keweenaw Connector Pipeline. Segment 1 runs from the Three Lakes Gate Station to valve site MLV1 near King Lake. Segment 2 runs from MLV1 to MLV2 at Alberta. Segment 3 runs from MLV2 to MLV3 at Six Mile Creek. Segment 4 runs from MLV3 to the Grist Mill Gate Station near Baraga.

There is no overview sheet, so the four sheets were stitched into one mosaic. All sheets share one scale and point north. Template matching of satellite patches between neighboring sheets gave pure translations that agreed across 10 to 17 patches per pair, each with correlation above 0.85. The offsets are in stitch_offsets.py and the mosaic is built by make_mosaic.py.

The pages are raster satellite images, not vector. The line is drawn in a different color on each sheet, so trace_segments.py extracted it by color mask, kept the largest component, thinned it to a one pixel path, and simplified it at about 1.2 pixels. A transmission line crossing on segment 3 was bridged by a small morphological closing. The four pieces meet at the valve pins with gaps of a few pixels, which were left as they are. overlay_trace.png shows the final trace over the mosaic, one color per segment, and the line is continuous from Three Lakes to the Grist Mill Gate Station.

The fit uses 12 ground control points, listed in gcps.json with a source reference each. Nine are lake centers, taken as the centroid of the Census TIGER/Line 2023 area water polygon and matched to the centroid of the dark water mask on the image: Summit, Kontio, Petticoat, Heart, Parent, Dommer, Ruth, King and Teddy Lakes. The other three are the southeast corner of the Houghton and Baraga county line (Census county file), the tip of the shore spit at Assinins in Keweenaw Bay (Census Lake Superior polygon vertex), and the junction of US 41, US 141 and M-28 at Alberta (end vertex of US Highway 141 in the Census roads file). No coordinate was read off the map image.

The first order fit has a root mean square error of 0.049 km, a leave-one-out error of 0.072 km, and a largest residual of 0.124 km. The limit for this row is 5 km. The report is georef_report.json and the output is fetched_layers/P8241_traced.geojson.

The traced length is 47.88 km, or 29.75 miles. The stated length is 30.00 miles, about 48.3 km, so the ratio is 0.99. The start is 987 m from the Census Three Lakes place point, and the end is about 195 m from both M-38 and Grist Mill Road. The line stays in Baraga County. Census roads, lakes, the shoreline and the county line sit within about 100 m of the imagery in the mosaic, which supports the stitch. check_result.py holds the length and endpoint checks.

Doubts follow. Control points are sparse in the north middle of the route, so that stretch rests on the Alberta junction, the county line corner and the Assinins spit. The Alberta junction pixel lies under a road shield and is good to about 3 pixels, or 40 m. The lake pixel centers come from color masks and may be off by a few pixels. The plat scale is about 1:100,000, so the line is as drawn on the approved plat, and the commission allowed minor deviations in the final build. The order text is inconsistent about 16.75 miles along US 41 versus along a transmission line, so the plats were used instead of the text.
