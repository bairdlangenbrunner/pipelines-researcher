# P8110 Cloud Connector Pipeline trace notes

The source is Figure 1.8-1 on page 54 of the Plaquemines Expansion draft Resource Report 1, made by Burns and McDonnell and issued August 15, 2025 (FERC accession 20250822-5162, docket PF25-7-000). The file is maps/P8110/1A_PlaqExpn_RR01_082225_PUB.pdf, and the page was rendered at 250 dpi into map250.png in this folder. The page is a flat raster basemap with a drawn red line, so the line was extracted by color and not read from vector paths.

The traced line is the red Cloud Connector route only, from its north end in Richland Parish to the Plaquemines Expansion star. The blue North Perryville Extension of about 20 miles is not included. The red pixels were masked, thinned to a one pixel centerline, and the pieces between the white milepost circles were chained from north to south. Each milepost circle gap of about 20 pixels (about 5 km) is bridged with a straight line. The trace has 205 points and is in trace.json, made by trace_red.py and chain.py. The overlay images were checked by eye.

Thirteen ground control points are in gcps.json. Each longitude and latitude is a vertex of the US Census Bureau 2023 county boundary file (cb_2023_us_county_500k) where parish lines meet or turn sharply, and the source_ref on each point says so. Each pixel is the matching corner or junction of the gray parish outline drawn on the map, found on 6 times zoom tiles made by tiles.py. A rough alignment (icp.py, prelim.py, tri.py, corners.py) was used only to show where candidate vertices should sit so they could be found on the image. It does not enter the final fit. The points run from Franklin Parish in the north to the Jefferson and Orleans parish area in the southeast.

The fit is first order (affine) from scripts/georef.py. The root mean square error is 0.39 km, the maximum residual is 0.65 km, and the leave-one-out error is 0.51 km, against a limit of 6 km. The output is fetched_layers/P8110_traced.geojson with the report in georef_report.json.

The traced length is 462.3 km (287.3 mi) against 300 mi stated, a ratio of 0.96. The shortfall is expected because the line is drawn at 1 to 1,600,000 and smooths small bends, and because the last few kilometers next to the star are not in the trace. The check.py script confirms the line crosses these parishes in order: Richland, Franklin, Catahoula, Concordia, Avoyelles, St. Landry, Pointe Coupee, Iberville, Assumption, Lafourche, Terrebonne, Lafourche, Jefferson, Plaquemines. This matches the parishes named in the source.

Doubts. At this scale one pixel is about 270 m, so the line is only good to about 1 km and should be rated low or medium accuracy. The line stops about 5 km short of the star at the east end, because the short red stub there was lost in the thinning. The southern end passes through Terrebonne and Jefferson at parish borders, which is likely map smoothing near Houma and is not a real route change. The point t158 sits on a wiggly river boundary, so its pixel is the least certain. The three easternmost points carry a larger offset in the preview alignment, but their pixels were read directly from the image. The Healthy Gulf permit narrative gives only text, so no detailed alignment was found to compare against.
