## What this settles, and what it does not

**P1485 is corroborated, not suspect.** 532.00 km / 1020 mm / 8.70 bcm/y on the sheet
matches the documented line on all three axes. Only its StartYear1 (1985 vs a documented
1986 commissioning) is in question.

**The overlap hypothesis for P1481 is now specific.** КАБ's own compressor chain runs
Ananiv → Moldova (Sholdanesti, Drochia) → **Khotyn, Chernivtsi oblast** → Bohorodchany.
That is exactly the corridor Moldovatransgaz calls **АЧБ, «Ананьев–Черновцы–Богородчаны»,
1020 мм, 9,1 млрд м³/рік** — same diameter, near-same capacity. So P1481 «Ananjiv-
Bohorodchani» is plausibly the western portion of P1485 under the Moldovan operator's
name for the same physical pipe.

**But the arithmetic does not close.** 532 − 333 = 199 km for Kremenchuk→Ananiv, whereas
that leg is ~300 km straight-line. Do NOT confirm a duplicate on the name/diameter
signature alone — that is the Kazakh cluster-A error. PARTIAL_OVERLAP or UNRESOLVED is
the honest shape unless a source states the segmentation directly.

## Independent of the duplication question

**P1485's `Owner = Gazprom PJSC 100%` is an attribution defect.** This is a Ukrainian
trunk; the transmission system is state-owned and operated by the Gas TSO of Ukraine
since 2020-01-01. Gazprom owns no Ukrainian section. Stage it regardless of the verdict.
