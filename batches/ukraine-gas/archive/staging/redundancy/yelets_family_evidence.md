# Pre-fetched evidence — the Yelets corridor family (clusters H and F)

Gathered in the main loop 2026-08-15.

## Both cluster-H rows are SEPARATELY documented lines

- **«Єлець — Курськ — Диканька»** (uk.wikipedia has its own article):
  **1220 мм**, robochyi tysk **5,5 МПа**, commissioned **1984**. Runs Yelets (Lipetsk obl.,
  RU) → Kursk → Dykanka, where it meets the Dykanka compressor station and can be sent south
  down «Диканька – Кременчук – Кривий Ріг» or west along the Shebelynka–Kyiv corridor.
  Compressor stations: КС Єлець цех №2, КС Курськ цех №2, КС Черемисіново цех №2,
  КС Суми (commissioned January 1985).
  Refs: adm.rkursk.ru, the utg.ua chronology, and two e-gorod.ru regional gas-supply
  characterisations (Lipetsk and Kursk oblasts) — all via web.archive.org.
  → matches **P0775** on diameter (1220) and year (1984).

- **«Єлець — Курськ — Київ»**: commissioned **1982** (GEM P0776 says 1981).

**No length figure was found for either line**, so the 298.00 / 297.00 km pair on the sheet
remains unsourced in both directions. What IS established is that these are two distinct,
independently documented pipelines sharing the Yelets–Kursk corridor and then diverging —
corroborated by the geometry (they split at Kursk; see route_geometry_findings.md). The
near-identical stated lengths are a coincidence to explain, not evidence of a duplicate.

## Cluster F context

The Dykanka node is where «Єлець – Курськ – Диканька» hands off to
«Диканька – Кременчук – Кривий Ріг» (= **P1460**). So P0775 and P1460 are documented as
CONSECUTIVE lines meeting at Dykanka, which explains the shared node in the geometry without
making either a duplicate of **P0783** (Yelets–Kremenchuk–Kryvyi Rih).

Note also, from the КЗУ research (see kzu_evidence.md): КЗУ-1 was commissioned in 1970
together with «Єфремівка — Диканька — Київ», and КЗУ-2 in 1973 together with
«Шебелинка — Диканька — Київ». Dykanka is a major hub with several genuinely distinct lines
converging on it — the same reason the P1452/P1453/P1454 duplicate flag was already refuted
in the leg-1 sweep.
