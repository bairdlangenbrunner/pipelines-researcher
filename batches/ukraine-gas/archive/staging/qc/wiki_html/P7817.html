Taganrog-Mariupol-Berdyansk Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Таганрог-Мариуполь-Бердянск, Таганрог-Жданов-Бердянск) is a retired gas pipeline in Russia and Ukraine.

Location

The pipeline runs from Taganrog, Rostov Oblast, Russia, through Zhdanov (currently, Mariupol), Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, and to Berdyansk, Zaporozhia Oblast, Ukraine.

Background

The original pipeline was retired in 2009.[3] However, after the self-proclaimed Donetsk People's Republic seceded from Ukraine in 2014 and was annexed by Russia in 2022, the local authorities has proposed to revive the Soviet-era pipeline.[5] Satellite images from July 2024 show that Russia is possibly constructing the replacement line of the Taganrog-Mariupol segment.[6] Additionally, in an official document from 2025, the Donetsk People's Republic Ministry of Construction stated that the replacement of the Taganrog-Mariupol segment is underway, whereas the Mariupol-Berdyansk segment is in the proposal stage.[7]
