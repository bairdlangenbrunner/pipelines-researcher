Punga-Ukhta-Gryazovets Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Пунга — Ухта — Грязовец III, Пунга — Ухта — Грязовец IV,) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.

Location

The pipeline starts near Svetlyi, Khanty-Mansi Autonomus Okrug, Russia and runs to Gryazovets, Vologda Oblast, Russia.[1][2]

Background

Punga-Ukhta-Gryazovets III runs from Svetlyi, Khanty-Mansi Autonomus Okrug, to Ukhta, Komi Republic, where it connects to Punga-Ukhta-Gryazovets IV, which ends in Gryazovets, Vologda Oblast.[7][10]
