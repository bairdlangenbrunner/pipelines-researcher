Kasimovskoye-Voskresensk Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Касимовское ПХГ - КС Воскресенск) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline starts at the Kasimovskoye Underground Gas Storage Facility (UGSF), Kasimovsky District, Ryazan Oblast, Russia, and runs to the Voskresensk Compressor Station, Kolomensky District, Moscow Oblast, Russia.[1][2]
