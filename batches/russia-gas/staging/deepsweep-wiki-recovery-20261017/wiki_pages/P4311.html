Yamburg-Tula Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Ямбург-Тула) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline starts in Yamburg, Nadymsky District, Yamalo-Nenets Autonomus Okrug and runs to Tula, Shchyokinsky District, Tula Oblast, Russia.[2][3]

Background

The pipeline was constructed in the 1980s by the Soviet Union. It was built in two phases, which are sometimes referred to as Yamburg-Tula 1 (Phase I)[2] and Yamburg-Tula 2 (Phase II).[6] These are two pipelines running parallel to each other.
