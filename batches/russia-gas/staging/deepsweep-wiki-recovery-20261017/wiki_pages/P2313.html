Gryazovets-Volkhov Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Грязовец - Волхов) is a gas pipeline under construction in Russia.[1][2][3]

Location

The pipeline is proposed to start in Gryazovetsky District, Vologda Oblast, Russia and to run to Volkhovsky District, Leningrad Oblast, Russia.[2]

Background

In January 2022, the construction of the gas pipeline pipes commenced.[5][6] The construction was expected to be completed in 2023.[8]

The pipeline is to supply gas to an LNG plant and a gas processing facility in Ust-Luga to produce LNG and other products such as polyethylene.[4]

Following the Russian invasion of Ukraine, the sanctions and the withdrawal of partners, including Linde GmbH, may have delayed the project.[9] Russia is considering alternatives such as domestic turbines or partnering with China.[10][11] As of July 2023, the status of the project is uncertain, with the latest update in December 2022 claiming that the LNG plant was in construction.[10]

With the Nord Stream pipelines mothballed, there is a surplus of gas in the region, which may also lead to a delay for the Gryazovets-Volkhov Gas Pipeline.

In February 2024, testing of the road surface was performed on the pipeline indicating that the construction was nearing completion.[7]

As of August 2025, the construction remains incomplete, but the project is assumed to be ongoing since the associated LNG plant is still expected to begin operations in 2027-2030.[12]
