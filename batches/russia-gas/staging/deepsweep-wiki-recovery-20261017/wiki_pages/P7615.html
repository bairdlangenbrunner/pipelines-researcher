Nizhnevartovsk-Parabel-Kuzbass Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод "Нижневартовский газоперерабатывающий завод (ГПЗ) - Парабель - Кузбасс") is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

Nizhnevartovsk-Parabel-Kuzbass I Gas Pipeline

The pipeline starts in Nizhnevartovsk, Khanty-Mansi Autonomous District, Russia and runs to Nizhenvartovsk, Kemerovo region, Russia.[2]

Nizhnevartovsk-Parabel-Kuzbass II Gas Pipeline

The pipeline starts in Vertikos, Tomsk region, Russia and runs to Nizhenvartovsk, Kemerovo region, Russia.[2]

Project Details

Nizhnevartovsk-Parabel-Kuzbass I Gas Pipeline

  • Operator: Gazprom Transgaz Tomsk LLC[3][4]
  • Owner: Gazprom[4]
  • Parent company: Gazprom PJSC[5]
  • Capacity: 8.2 bcm/year (total for the pipeline)[4]
  • Length: 1162 km[4]
  • Diameter: 1020 mm,[6] 1420 mm[7]
  • Status: Operating[3]
  • Start Year: 1978[8]
  • Cost:
  • Financing:
  • Associated infrastructure: Samotlorskoye Oil and Gas Field, Volodino-Krasnoyarsk Gas Pipeline, Myldzhino-Vertikos Gas Pipeline

Nizhnevartovsk-Parabel-Kuzbass II Gas Pipeline

  • Operator: Gazprom Transgaz Tomsk LLC[3]
  • Owner: Gazprom[4]
  • Parent company: Gazprom PJSC[5]
  • Capacity: 8.2 bcm/year (total for the pipeline)[4]
  • Length: 714 km[4][9]
  • Diameter: 700 mm[10]
  • Status: Operating[3][11]
  • Start Year: 2012 (Vertikos-Belovo segment), 2017 (Belovo-Novokuznetsk segment)[10]
  • Cost:
  • Financing:
  • Associated infrastructure: Samotlorskoye Oil and Gas Field, Volodino-Krasnoyarsk Gas Pipeline, Myldzhino-Vertikos Gas Pipeline
