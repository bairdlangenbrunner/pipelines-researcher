SRTO-Surgut-Omsk Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод "СРТО - Сургут - Омск", "Северные районы Тюменской области - Сургут - Омск" ) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1][2]

Location

The pipeline starts in Noyabrsk, Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous District, and runs to Omsk, Omsk region, Russia.[3][4]

Project Details

  • Operator: Gazprom Transgaz Tomsk LLC[2], Gazprom Transgaz Surgut LLC[5]
  • Owner: Gazprom PJSC[2]
  • Parent company: Gazprom PJSC[6]
  • Capacity: 32.8 bcm/y[5]
  • Length: 763 km[7]
  • Diameter: 1,400 mm[8], 1220 mm[5]
  • Status: Operating[2][5]
  • Start Year: 1988[7]

  • Associated infrastructure: Omsk-Novosibirsk-Kuzbass Gas Pipeline, SRTO-Surgut Omsk Gas Pipeline

Background

In July 2023, a section of the pipeline underwent repaired as a result of a failure discovered during the diagnostics of the system .[9]
