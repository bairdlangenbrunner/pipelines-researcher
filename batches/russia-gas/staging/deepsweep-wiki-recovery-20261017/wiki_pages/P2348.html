Nadym-Punga Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Надым - Пунга) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline starts in Nadymsky District, Yamalo-Nenets Autonomus Okrug, Russia and runs to Beryozovsky District, Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug, Russia.[1]

Background

The pipeline connects to the Medvezhye-Nadym Gas Pipeline and sources gas from the Medvezhye Oil and Gas Field.[4]
