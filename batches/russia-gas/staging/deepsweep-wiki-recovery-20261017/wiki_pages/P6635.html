Vologda-Cherepovets Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод-отвод Вологда - Череповец) is a proposed gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline is proposed to run from Spasskoye to Tonshalovo, Vologda region, Russia.[2]

Background

The project was included in the list of "Planned Transmission Gas Pipelines", released by the Ministry of Energy of Russia in May 2015 and revised in May 2025.[2] The pipeline is a branch of the Gryazovets-Leningrad Gas Pipeline and starts in the Sheksninsky district, Vologda region.[2][6] In January 2024, it was reported that the front-end engineering design of the pipeline was in progress.[7] As of August 2025, there have not been any updates about the project.
