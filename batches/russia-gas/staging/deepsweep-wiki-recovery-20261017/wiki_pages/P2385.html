Saratov-Moscow Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Саратов - Москва) is a retired gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline used to start in Saratov, Saratov Oblast, Russia and run to Moscow, Moscow Oblast, Russia.[1]
