Srednebotuobinskoye-Novolenskaya CHP Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод "УКПГ СБНГКМ-Новоленская ТЭС") is a proposed gas pipeline in Russia.

Location

The pipeline will start at the Srednebotuobinskoye Gas and Condensate Field, Sakha Republic, and run to Novolenskaya CHP power station near Lensk, Sakha Republic, Russia.

Background

In 2024, the project was included in the list of "Planned Transmission Gas Pipelines" released by the Ministry of Energy of Russia.[3] The pipeline will be used to supply gas from the Srednebotuobinskoye Gas and Condensate Field to the Novolenskaya gas station, which is under construction 15 kilometers away from Lensk in the Sakha Republic.[1][5] Public consultations on the project took place in April 2025.[7]
