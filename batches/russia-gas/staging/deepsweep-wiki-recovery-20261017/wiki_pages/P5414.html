Komsomolskoe-Surgut-Chelyabinsk Gas Pipeline (Russian: Магистральный газопровод Комсомольское-Сургут-Челябинск) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.

Location

The pipeline starts in Komsomolskoe, Yamalo-Nenets Autonomus Okrug, Russia and runs to Chelyabinsk, Chelyabinsk Oblast, Russia.

Background

The Komsomolskoe-Surgut-Chelyabinsk Gas Pipeline and the Urengoy-Chelyabinsk Gas Pipeline form a two-pipeline gas transportation corridor, which is used to deliver gas from the gas fields in the Yamal-Nenets Autonomous Okrug to the Trans-Urals, the Sverdlovsk, Chelyabinsk and Orenburg Oblast, and the Republic of Bashkortostan.[5]

The pipeline seem to run parallel to each other.[6][2] The route is copied from the Urengoy-Chelyabinsk Gas Pipeline, with the start point altered to a Purpe compressor station near the Komsomolskoe gas field.[7][8]
