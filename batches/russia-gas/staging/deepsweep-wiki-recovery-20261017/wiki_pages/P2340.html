Minnibaevo-Kazan Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Миннибаево - Казань) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline starts in Minnibaevo, Republic of Tatarstan, Russia and runs to Kazan, Republic of Tatarstan, Russia.[1]

Background

Environment and social impacts

Gazprom transgaz Kazan required the demolition of dozens of houses in a suburban corner of Kazan in 2020. The houses violate the 150 meters gas pipeline safety zone. There has been no communication about the violation since the commissioning of the pipeline until now.[6]
