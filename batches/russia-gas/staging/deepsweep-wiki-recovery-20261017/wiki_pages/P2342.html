Mokrous-Samara-Tolyatti Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопровод Мокроус - Самара - Тольятти) is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline starts in Mokrous, Saratov Oblast, Russia and runs to Tolyatti, Samara Oblast, Russia.[1]
