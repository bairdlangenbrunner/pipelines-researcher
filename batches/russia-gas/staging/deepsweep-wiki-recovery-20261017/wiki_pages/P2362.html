Omsk-Novosibirsk-Kuzbass Gas Pipeline (Russian: Газопроводе" Омск-Новосибирск-Кузбасс") is an operating gas pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline starts in Omsk, Omsk region, and runs to Yurginsky district, Kemerovo region, Russia.[1][2]

Loading map...

Omsk-Novosibirsk Gas Pipeline

The pipeline runs from Omsk, Omsk region, to Novosibirsk, Novosibirsk region, Russia.[2]

Loading map...

Novosibirsk-Kuzbass Gas Pipeline

The pipeline runs from Novosibirsk, Novosibirsk region, to the Proskokovo Compressor Station in Yurginsky District, Kemerovo region, Russia.[2]
