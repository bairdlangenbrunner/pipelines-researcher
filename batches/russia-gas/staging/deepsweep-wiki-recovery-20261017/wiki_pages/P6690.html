Zapadno-Ozernoye–Anadyr Gas Pipeline (Russian: магистральный газопровод "Газопровод "Западно-­Озерное ГМ – г. Анадырь") is an operating condensate pipeline in Russia.[1]

Location

The pipeline runs from the Zapadno-Ozernoye Gas Field, Chukotka Autonomus District, to Anadyr, Chukotka Autonomus District, Russia.[1]

Background

The pipeline starts at the Zapadno-Ozernoye Gas Field and ends at the Andyr Gas Distribution Station in Anadyr.[1] The pipeline route to the city of Anadyr is laid almost straight to the northeast from the Zapadno-Ozernaya area.[4]
